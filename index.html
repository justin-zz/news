A few things that help when phrasing a question, whether here or in any search bar:
  • Name a source type — "what do wire services report on X" tends to surface more neutral, primary reporting than a generic search.
  • Ask what's disputed — "how is X being covered differently" or "what's disputed about X" surfaces multiple viewpoints instead of one blended answer.
  • Anchor recency — adding "as of today" or "this week" favors fresher coverage over older, higher-ranking pages.
  • Separate fact from rumor — "what has been officially confirmed about X, and what is still unverified" produces more appropriately hedged answers on breaking stories.
  • Two-step it — ask the plain question first, then a narrower follow-up ("what's disputed about this") rather than cramming both into one query.
